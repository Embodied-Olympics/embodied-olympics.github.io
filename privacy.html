ro-live privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What the tool does

ro-live uses YouTube API Services. With the permission of the channel owner, it creates, starts and ends live broadcasts and live streams on the owner's channel, reads the health and viewer count of those broadcasts, and uploads videos to that channel.

What data it uses and stores

The tool keeps the owner's OAuth tokens and stream key in a private file on the owner's own computers. It does not send them to anyone else. It does not collect, store or share data about viewers or about any other person. It has no analytics, no cookies and no advertising.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, the stored tokens stop working, and the owner can delete the file that holds them.

Google and YouTube

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
